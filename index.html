💡 What is a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest a fixed sum regularly into mutual funds. It inculcates financial discipline, removes market timing emotion, and leverages Rupee Cost Averaging—buying more fund units when prices are low and fewer units when prices are high.

🚀 Why Step-Up (Top-Up) SIP is a Game Changer

Most people keep their SIP amount constant for 15 years even though their salary increases annually by 8-15%. By stepping up your SIP by just 10% annually, you can often accumulate 2x to 3x more wealth over a 20-year career without feeling any financial pinch!

📉 The Hidden Danger of Inflation

₹1 Crore in 20 years sounds like a lot of money, but at 6% annual inflation, ₹1 Crore in 20 years will have the purchasing power of only ~₹31 Lakhs today. That's why our calculator includes real purchasing power analysis so you plan realistically.

📐 SIP Calculation Mathematical Formula

The mutual fund industry calculates SIP maturity using the Annuity Due compound interest formula:
M = P × [((1 + i)ⁿ - 1) / i] × (1 + i) Where P is monthly installment, i is periodic monthly rate, and n is total months.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the minimum amount required to start a SIP? ▼

Most mutual fund houses in India permit starting a SIP with as little as ₹500 or ₹1,000 per month. Some micro-SIP schemes even start at ₹100 per month.

Can I stop, pause, or change my SIP amount anytime? ▼

Yes! SIPs are completely flexible. There are no penalty charges for pausing or modifying an ongoing SIP in open-ended mutual funds.

What is the average expected return for equity mutual funds in India? ▼

Historically over 10-15 year periods, Indian broad market indices like the Nifty 50 and BSE Sensex have compounded between 12% to 14% CAGR, while actively managed mid-cap and small-cap funds have generated 14% to 18% CAGR.
